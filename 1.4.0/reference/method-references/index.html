    Method References and Reporting

    Use these references for the scientific background of an analysis. The linked workflow pages define the model and options implemented in PCS Analyzer; a reference to a general method is not evidence that every variant is supported.

    PCS and susceptibility conventions

    The point-dipole PCS expression and its extension to distributed spin density are discussed in A partial differential equation for pseudocontact shift. For broader context, see Magnetic susceptibility and paramagnetism-based NMR.

    For calculations in this application, use the definitions in PCS Theory and Tensor Conventions: coordinates in Å, anisotropies in 10⁻³² m³ per molecule and output shifts in ppm. Check axial/rhombic definitions and frame conventions before comparing published tensors. The PCS Workbench describes the separate point/distributed-field comparison and its grid assumptions.

    Lanthanide series and Reilley separation

    • Bleaney's Nuclear magnetic resonance shifts in solution due to lanthanide ions provides the historical lanthanide-shift framework.
    • Separation of contact and dipolar lanthanide induced nuclear magnetic resonance shifts: evaluation and application of some structure independent methods is an original-method reference for contact/dipolar separation.
    • Pinkerton et al. (1985), Table 1, is the source identified for the RHF spin-factor coefficients.
    • The DTMA fluoride-binding study is an application example, not the original source of the separation method.

    The Multi-Lanthanide tutorial states the application's relative C_D convention, temperature expression, fixed Sm/Eu approximation and common-temperature requirements. Report those choices when interpreting separated components. A linear trend alone does not establish unchanged geometry or contact coupling across the series.

    Joint PCS and RDC

    The general relationship between susceptibility anisotropy and paramagnetic NMR observables, including RDC, is covered by the susceptibility review. The Joint PCS+RDC tutorial specifies the narrower implemented model: magnetic self-alignment, a fixed structure/frame, two fitted tensor scalars and the stated residual weighting. RDC from a separate alignment medium is outside that model.

    PRE

    The PRE tutorial gives the actual proton Γ₂ equation and omitted terms. Compare it with the Xplor-NIH PRE model description and the relaxation-model discussion, equations 12–14. Report the magnetic field, moment, correlation-time bounds and errors, and explain why the simplified model is appropriate for the sample. An ion preset or a fitted effective time alone does not establish that appropriateness.

    Reliability evaluation

    SciPy's bootstrap documentation explains percentile resampling intervals. The grouped cross-validation documentation and data-leakage guidance provide statistical background for keeping related observations together and withheld data out of training. These are method explanations, not additional PCS Analyzer dependencies or a claim that its evaluator calls those APIs.

    The Multi-Conformer tutorial defines the implemented parametric/cases bootstrap, grouping, failed-refit handling and held-out score. Its intervals are conditional on the supplied geometry, mapping, orientation and model; they do not propagate uncertainty in those inputs.

    Report a reproducible analysis

    Alongside the software version and archived-release citation, retain:

    • original structure and experimental data, acquisition/reference conditions, assignments and any excluded observations with reasons;
    • tensor units/frame, averaging assumptions and fitted versus fixed parameters;
    • initialization, bounds, objective weights and diagnostic results;
    • a saved project and numerical result exports; NMR work also needs its session;
    • for reliability evaluation, resampling method, group definition, seed, requested and successful refit counts, and failed/partial evaluation status.

    Use the file-format reproducibility checklist for the supported exports. A screenshot alone does not retain a complete fit.
